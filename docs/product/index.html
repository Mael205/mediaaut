Six stages, all offline.

They run in order, and each writes its output to disk — so a failure in the last one never costs you the first five.

1

It writes the script

A local model drafts to a measured word budget, because a script written without one produces a video twice its target length. Invented percentages and speed multipliers are stripped before they ever reach the voice.

2

It speaks

Voice cloned from a sample you provide, synthesised in sentence groups — never in one block, where autoregressive drift turns the last sentence to mush and nothing in the output signals it.

3

It subtitles, word by word

Whisper timestamps every word on the GPU. Type size is measured per font rather than derived from font metrics, which are wrong by 15–30%. That is why each cue fills the frame instead of floating in it.

4

It illustrates what is said

Visuals anchor to the spoken sentence instead of being dealt out in turn. Stock photos are ranked against that sentence by CLIP in 154 MB of quantised ONNX — with no PyTorch install anywhere.

5

It renders

ffmpeg with hardware encoding, subtitles burned during the encode: no browser, no frame-by-frame capture. Loudness lands at -14 LUFS, where every platform normalises playback anyway.

6

It publishes, then checks

YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It reads the platform back after upload rather than trusting the response, and paces posts so a burst never trips a spam filter.

Long-form too: 16:9, chaptered, with a generated thumbnail — plus a cutter that turns one long video into six verticals.

Questions people actually ask.

Do I need a powerful machine?

An NVIDIA GPU makes it comfortable — the log above is one desktop card turning out a 48-second video in 91 seconds. It runs on CPU, more slowly. Everything else is ffmpeg, which runs anywhere.

Is anything sent to a server?

Only what you ask for: stock footage searches, and the upload itself when you publish. Writing, voice, transcription and rendering never leave the machine.

Will AI-made video get my channel demonetised?

Platforms act against mass-produced, templated content with no original input — not against AI assistance. mediaaut rotates layouts between videos and holds each channel to a stated editorial angle, which is what that distinction actually turns on.

Can I use my own voice?

Yes. Drop a clean sample into the voices folder and the narration is cloned from it.

What happens when I get stuck?

You email, and the person who wrote it answers. There is no support tier.
